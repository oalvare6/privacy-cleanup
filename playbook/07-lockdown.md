# Phase 7: Lockdown checklist

The user does these steps themselves, because they involve their SSN, bank and carrier logins. Your job is to walk them through each one, give the exact link, and tick it off in `WHERE-I-STAND.md`. Everything here is free.

Adapt the list to the user's country. This version is US-focused.

## Phone (SIM-swap protection)

A published phone number is the biggest risk: someone can move the number to their own SIM and take over accounts that use text codes.
- At the carrier, turn on **SIM protection** and **port-out protection** (sometimes called a number lock or transfer PIN) for every line. Only the account owner can do this, so on a family plan that may be a parent.
- Set a new random account PIN, not a birthday, an address or phone digits.
- Opt out of the carrier's data sharing and ad targeting in its privacy settings.
- Register at donotcall.gov, plus the state list if there is one.

## Logins

- **Email first:** add a passkey or authenticator app, check the recovery email and phone, and sign out of unknown devices.
- Do the same for the bank, the carrier, Apple or Google, any crypto or brokerage accounts, and dev or cloud accounts.
- Use a password manager (Apple Passwords and Google Password Manager are free). Change any password that was in a breach or reused.
- Review third-party app access on Google, GitHub and social accounts, and revoke anything unused.

## Credit and identity

- **Freeze credit** at Equifax, Experian and TransUnion.
- Optional extra freezes: **Innovis**, **NCTUE** (stops fake phone and utility accounts), **ChexSystems** (stops fake bank accounts), **LexisNexis**.
- **IRS Identity Protection PIN:** irs.gov/ippin
- Create a **my Social Security** account at ssa.gov/myaccount before someone else does.
- **USPS Informed Delivery** at the current address.
- Opt out of prescreened credit offers: optoutprescreen.com
- Free breach alerts: haveibeenpwned.com/NotifyMe
- Pull free credit reports at annualcreditreport.com and look for accounts the user doesn't recognize. If there are any, go to identitytheft.gov.

## Finish

Record what's done and what's left in `WHERE-I-STAND.md`.
